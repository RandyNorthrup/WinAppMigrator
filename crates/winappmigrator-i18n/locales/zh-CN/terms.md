# zh-CN terms

Simplified Chinese as Windows 11 writes it for mainland China: 你 (not 您), as Windows 10 and 11 address the reader;
Microsoft's 帐户 and 撤消; full-width punctuation (，。：；（）“”) in Chinese text; a space between Chinese and Latin
words, digits, and placeholders, as Windows writes “Windows 设置” and “64 位”.
