# Thai terms (th-TH)

Thai as Windows 11 writes it: polite and neutral, "คุณ" only where a "you" is needed, "โปรด" for a request, no ครับ/ค่ะ.
No full stop at the end of a sentence; sentences are parted by a space. A space goes before and after Latin words,
numbers, and placeholders. Quotation marks are “ ”. Digits stay 0 to 9.

The program breaks lines only at spaces, so a long sentence also takes a space between its phrases, where a Thai
writer would pause: before และ, หรือ, เนื่องจาก, จนกว่า, a relative ที่, or the verb after a long subject. Keep each
unbroken run under about 250 pixels (roughly 35 letters) so it fits the narrowest panel.
