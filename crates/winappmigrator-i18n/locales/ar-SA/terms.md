# Arabic (ar-SA) terms

Form of address: the second person singular, with imperatives for actions ("اختر", "أدخل", "انقر فوق"), as Windows
writes Arabic ("هل تريد السماح لهذا التطبيق بإجراء تغييرات على جهازك؟"). Passive and impersonal forms ("تم", "تعذر",
"يتم") where English says what happened. Digits stay 0 to 9. Comma "،", semicolon "؛", quotation marks "".
