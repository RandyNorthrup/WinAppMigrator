# Korean terms (ko-KR)

Style: Windows 11 Korean. Statements end in 합니다체 (-습니다/-ㅂ니다); instructions in -하세요; buttons, titles, and
labels are noun phrases. The reader is never called 당신; the sentence leaves the subject out, as Windows does. A reason
inserted after a colon is a polite clause with no full stop of its own. Particles after a placeholder use Microsoft's
을(를), 이(가), 은(는), 와(과), (으)로, or a noun is put after the placeholder (파일, 폴더, 서비스) so the particle is known.
