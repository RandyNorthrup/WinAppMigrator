# Terms: English → français (fr-FR)

Address: *vous*, imperative in the *vous* form, as Windows 11 in French. Sentence case for titles, labels, and buttons.
Typography: a no-break space (U+00A0) before `:` `;` `?` `!`, inside `« »`, and between a number and its unit; straight
apostrophe `'`; `…` in the windows, `...` in the console client (Windows 7 consoles lack `…`).
