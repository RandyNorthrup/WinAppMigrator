# Urdu (ur-PK) terms

Form of address: آپ, with the polite imperative ending in ـیں ("منتخب کریں", "دبائیں", "دوبارہ کوشش کریں"), as Windows
writes Urdu. Passive forms ("نہیں کیا جا سکا", "رکھ دیا گیا") where English says what happened. Urdu letters ی ے ہ ھ ک,
never Arabic ي ه ك. Full stop "۔", comma "،", semicolon "؛", question mark "؟", quotation marks "". Digits 0 to 9.
Product names, flags, paths, codes and "PC" stay in Latin script, inline.
