# واژه‌نامه فارسی (fa-IR)

Form of address: «شما» and the polite plural imperative («انتخاب کنید»، «وارد کنید»), as Windows 11 writes Persian.
Letters ی and ک (never Arabic ي and ك); zero-width non-joiner in می‌، نمی‌، ‌ها، ‌های، ‌شده and compounds. Persian
punctuation: comma «،», semicolon «؛», quotation marks « ». Digits stay 0 to 9. Product names, paths, flags and
placeholders stay in Latin script, inline. Fragments inserted after a colon or into another sentence have no full stop.
Plurals: fa has `one` and `other`; a noun after a number stays singular («3 فایل»).
