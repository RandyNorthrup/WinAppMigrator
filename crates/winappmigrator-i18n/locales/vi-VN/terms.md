# Thuật ngữ tiếng Việt (vi-VN)

Form of address: "bạn", as Windows 11 in Vietnamese writes it. Sentence case on titles, labels, and buttons. Quotation
marks “ ”. Windows' own names are as Windows 11 in Vietnamese shows them.
