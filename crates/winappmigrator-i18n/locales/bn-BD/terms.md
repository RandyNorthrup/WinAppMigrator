## Style

- The reader is "আপনি", with the polite command form (করুন, বেছে নিন), as Windows does in Bengali.
- Buttons are commands (ইনস্টল করুন); titles and labels are noun phrases. Bengali has no capital letters.
- A sentence ends with "।". A fragment that goes into another sentence has none.
- Quotation marks: “ ”. Digits stay 0 to 9.
- A case ending after a placeholder is joined with a hyphen: { $path }-এ, { $name }-এর.
- Plurals use bn's CLDR forms `one` and `other`. A count takes "টি": { $count }টি ফাইল.
- Written for readers in Bangladesh, in Bangla Academy spelling: plural noun ending -গুলো (not -গুলি); কোনো, কখনো,
  এখনো, তখনো; সেবা for a service (not পরিষেবা); সার্টিফিকেট for a certificate (not শংসাপত্র).
