# Hindi (hi-IN) terms

Form of address: आप, with the polite imperative (चुनें, करें), as Windows in Hindi writes. Full stop: पूर्ण विराम (।).
Digits 0 to 9. Product names, flags, paths, and codes stay in Latin script.
